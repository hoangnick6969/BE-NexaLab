- (direction) Content Foundation is a B1 enablement workstream; teachers do not author the core Grade 8-9 corpus.
- (capability) CAP-1 traceable curriculum-aligned packs separate mapped outcomes, reviewed knowledge, released lessons, practicals, and simulations.
- (capability) CAP-2 private versioned R2 media is referenced by PostgreSQL metadata and immutable released asset versions.
- (capability) CAP-3 SourceRegistry ingestion stages allowlisted candidates and never auto-publishes.
- (capability) CAP-4 simulations require reviewed activity/model/golden cases and immutable model/visual/binding/challenge versions.
- (capability) CAP-5 open Grade 8-9 KHTN baseline uses original Vietnamese writing and verified reusable sources.
- (constraint) Official curriculum is taxonomy/reference, not permission to reproduce textbooks; unclear, NC, ND, or publisher-viewer content is excluded.
- (constraint) Synthetic fixtures may unblock software implementation but can never be promoted as baseline content.
- (question) Q-DATA-01 media budget, Q-DATA-02 initial flagship topics, Q-DATA-03 named content/review capacity, and Q-DATA-04 coverage thresholds gate affected releases.
- (event) Coherence validation passed; platform contracts are implementable while corpus and per-asset releases retain explicit gates.
- (event) Preservation validation passed against content-data-foundation, plan decisions D16-D24, PRD, and architecture spine.
