topic: TAI-05 AI credits ledger with estimate, reserve, settle and refund for Teacher AI jobs
updated: 2026-11-05T21:12
---

- (direction) Parent asked for TAI-05 spec: per-teacher CreditAccount, immutable double-entry-style ledger, PricingVersion per purpose, estimate/reserve/settle/refund keyed by AiJob.id, admin manual grants, no payment gateway, reconciliation script.
- (note) Brownfield: AiJob (TAI-01) records inputTokens/outputTokens, status and failureCode; quota is checked under a per-actor advisory lock in the create transaction; IdempotencyRecord and audit() exist; Gemini free tier has zero provider cost in pilot.
- (capability) CAP-1 teacher sees estimated credits before submitting and must confirm the estimate; success: create fails with AI_ESTIMATE_CHANGED when the confirmed value differs from the server estimate.
- (capability) CAP-2 reserve at job creation and settle or refund at terminal status, atomically with the job transition; success: every terminal job has exactly one reserve and exactly one settle or refund.
- (capability) CAP-3 balance never negative; success: concurrent creates at the boundary cannot overdraw, enforced in the same locked transaction as quota.
- (capability) CAP-4 admin grants and signed adjustments with reason, idempotency and audit; success: adjustments that would make available balance negative are rejected.
- (capability) CAP-5 teacher credit view shows available, reserved and history in credits only; success: no token counts or provider names in teacher responses.
- (capability) CAP-6 reconciliation script proves ledger integrity; success: exits non-zero on any unbalanced transaction, cached balance drift or job without settlement.
- (decision) Ledger: CreditTransaction header + CreditEntry rows with signed integer amounts summing to zero per transaction; accounts TEACHER_AVAILABLE, TEACHER_RESERVED per teacher and SYSTEM_ISSUANCE, SYSTEM_CONSUMED singletons.
- (decision) CreditAccount.balance is a cached projection updated in the same transaction with a CHECK balance >= 0 on teacher accounts; entries are the source of truth.
- (decision) Unique (aiJobId, kind) for RESERVE, SETTLE, REFUND plus a check that SETTLE and REFUND are mutually exclusive per job, enforced in code under the job row lock.
- (decision) Charge = min(reserved estimate, token cost) * validCount / requestedCount, rounded up to whole credits; never above the confirmed estimate.
- (decision) Any FAILED job and any CANCELLED job refunds in full, including teacher cancel after the provider call; absorbed cost is visible in usage reports.
- (decision) Credits complement TAI-01 quota: daily and concurrency quota remain abuse and provider-rate guards; credits are the budget. Both checked in the same create transaction.
- (decision) PricingVersion per purpose: baseCredits, perItemCredits, creditsPer1kInputTokens, creditsPer1kOutputTokens; jobs pin pricingVersionId at reserve; activation retires the previous version for new jobs only.
- (decision) Feature flag AI_CREDITS_ENABLED; when false TAI-01 behaves exactly as specified without credits.
- (constraint) No payment gateway, top-up purchase, invoices or refunds to money; credits have no cash value.
- (constraint) Gate: prices are set only after >=1 month of TAI-01 measured token usage recorded in a pricing evidence note.
- (question) What initial grant does each pilot teacher receive, and who approves grants?
- (question) Do credits expire or roll over monthly?
- (decision) Tickets TAI5-T0..T6.
- (decision) Estimate = baseCredits + perItemCredits * requestedCount so the server can recompute it at create; token rates only affect the charge.
- (decision) AiJob gains pricingVersionId, estimatedCredits, chargedCredits (expand-only); settlement has no route and runs inside TAI-01 terminal transitions.
- (event) Coherence validation passed: six capabilities with intent+success, constraints rule out overdraft, overcharge, payment and token exposure, success signal testable with failure injection.
- (event) Preservation validation passed against the parent directive and TAI-01 roadmap TAI-05 row: ledger, PricingVersion, estimate/reserve/settle/refund, idempotency, refund-on-failure, admin grants, reconciliation and one-month gate preserved.
- (gap by user) Sequencing conflict remains open: product direction expects an estimate and credit reserve before each teacher-facing AI operation, while TAI-01 excludes credits and TAI-05 waits for one month of usage to calibrate prices. Decide between a restricted internal no-credit pilot and moving the minimum estimate/reserve/refund path before teacher launch; admin grants only and no real-money payment initially.
